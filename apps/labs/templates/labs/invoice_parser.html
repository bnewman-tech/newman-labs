Extraction starts automatically when you choose or drop a PDF and enter the processing access code. Documents are processed by Prefect Managed, DocFirewall, Docling, Neon, Pydantic AI Gateway, its model provider, and Pydantic Logfire. The document record and private parser artifacts are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. The transient extraction handoff is deleted after delivery and no invoice-analysis record is retained.
